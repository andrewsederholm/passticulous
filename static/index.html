Passticulous

Strong passwords and passphrases, generated in your browser.

Characters
Style

Words come from the EFF large wordlist (7,776 words, ~12.9 bits each).

Build a password like Giraffe3287#@!: a themed word, then numbers, then symbols.

Allowed symbols

Patterns are easy to remember but weaker than a fully random password. Check the strength meter, and add numbers, symbols or words to make it stronger.

Everything is generated locally with crypto.getRandomValues. Nothing you generate is sent to or stored on the server.

Scripting? curl -s 'http://localhost/api/generate?length=24&format=text'
